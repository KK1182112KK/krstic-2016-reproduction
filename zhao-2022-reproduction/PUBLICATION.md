## Committed versus regenerated

Committed: unchanged physical-plant/predictor runner, unchanged tests, 18-case compact numerical CSV, 9-case literal-composition CSV, test log, report, requirements and Colab wrapper.

Regenerated by `python audit.py --out regenerated-results`: all 18 full trajectory CSVs, per-case JSON, full summaries, algebra/arithmetic diagnostics, source/environment hashes and own simulation plots/transport reconstructions.

Not committed: original paper PDFs, source-page renders, author-generated plots, digitized source curves, full prior trajectory CSVs, binary NPZ/PNG outputs, prior PDF report and its complete vector-comparison tooling. These remain in the separately supplied archive; the numerical runner itself does not require a paper PDF.
