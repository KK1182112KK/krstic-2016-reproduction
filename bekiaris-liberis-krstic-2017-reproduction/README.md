# Bekiaris-Liberis & Krstic (2017): multi-input nonlinear predictor reproduction

Independent equation-to-simulation audit for Kenshin Kotari. Development and reporting were AI-assisted. Original theory, models, equations and published figures remain attributed to Nikolaos Bekiaris-Liberis and Miroslav Krstic.

**Read [REPORT.md](REPORT.md) before interpreting the results.** The audit directly advances the original delayed nonholonomic-unicycle plant, reconstructs both predictors from issued-input history, checks the identities `P_i(t)=X(t+D_i)` only after the physical run, and keeps sampled/ZOH implementation effects separate from continuous-time theory.

Source: *Predictor-Feedback Stabilization of Multi-Input Nonlinear Systems*, IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control 62(2), 516–531 (2017), DOI 10.1109/TAC.2016.2558293.

## Main measured conclusion

For the published unicycle example with `D1=0.5`, `D2=1`, the second-predictor identity residual decreases by approximately a factor of two when the controller sampling period is halved, from about `5.42e-3` at 0.04 s to `3.35e-4` at 0.0025 s. The compensated simulation is therefore numerically consistent with the implemented predictor construction. Startup compatibility and the late uncompensated-figure discrepancy are reported separately; neither is silently folded into a claim that the entire paper is false or fully verified.
