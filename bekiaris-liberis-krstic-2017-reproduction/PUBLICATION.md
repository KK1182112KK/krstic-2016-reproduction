## Committed versus regenerated

Committed: physical-plant/predictor runner, independent uncompensated reference, postprocessing code, tests, Markdown report, a repository-based Colab wrapper, and compact numerical JSON evidence.

Regenerated by the runners: all 17 main full-trajectory CSVs, the three full continuous-reference trajectories, further full trajectory/refinement tables, and binary plots. The supplied audit ZIP retains those large raw trajectories. They are not duplicated in this parent repository because they exceed 100 MB in aggregate and are deterministically reproducible from the committed source.

Not redistributed: original paper PDF, author-generated figures, digitized published-curve CSVs, or source-curve overlay images. The separate full audit archive retains the optional figure-comparison tooling and source-curve extracts used during the local audit.

The notebook in this public folder is a repository-based re-execution wrapper; it was not executed on the Google Colab service. No MATLAB, GitHub Actions, author-code access, formal theorem certification, or misconduct claim is made.
